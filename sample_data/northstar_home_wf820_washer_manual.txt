NORTHSTAR HOME WF-820 FRONT-LOAD WASHER
FICTIONAL SAMPLE MANUAL — FOR THE MYPRODUCTCARE DEMO ONLY

ABOUT THIS SAMPLE
Northstar Home and model WF-820 are invented for a software demonstration. This
document is not an actual manufacturer's manual. Always follow the manual supplied
with a real appliance.

CAPACITY
The fictional WF-820 has a maximum laundry load of 8 kg. Do not exceed the capacity
listed on the rating label of a real washer.

WATER SUPPLY: ERROR 4C
In this sample, error 4C means the washer did not detect enough incoming water during
the fill cycle. Safely check that the household water tap is open and that the visible
inlet hose is not kinked. If the code remains, stop the cycle and contact an authorized
service professional. Do not remove covers or attempt electrical repairs.

DETERGENT DRAWER
If detergent residue builds up, remove the drawer only as described in the manual for
the real appliance and rinse it with warm water. Let it dry before putting it back.
Do not force a stuck drawer.

GENERAL CLEANING
Disconnect power before routine cleaning. Wipe the exterior with a soft damp cloth.
Do not use abrasive cleaners. Do not open the appliance or work on electrical parts.

WARRANTY
This sample manual does not state real warranty coverage. Check the original warranty
issued for the actual product for dates, exclusions, and claim instructions. The
separate warranty file in this demo is fictional too.
